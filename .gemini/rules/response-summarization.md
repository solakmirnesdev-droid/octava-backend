# Response Summarization & Executive Takeaways Standard

Every single response MUST conclude with a clear, concise, bulleted **Summary / TL;DR (Ukratko)** section at the very end.

## Requirements:
1. **Mandatory End-of-Turn Section**:
   - The final section of every response must be titled `### 📌 Ukratko (TL;DR):` or `### 📌 Summary:`.
2. **Concise Bullets (2–4 lines maximum)**:
   - Summarize the exact actions taken, key state/metrics, and the immediate next step or required user action.
3. **No Fluff**:
   - Deliver fast, high-impact bullet points so the user can quickly understand the outcome without reading long technical paragraphs.
